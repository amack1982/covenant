The Covenant of the Flowers

A novel, published online

What this site is

The Covenant of the Flowers is a work of fiction released chapter by chapter on this website. The main site is an interactive companion to the story: a map of its characters and their relationships, an atlas, a calendar, and a roadmap of the chapters as they are written.

The Compendium is the site’s e-reader. It holds the published chapters and articles, with optional music and visual effects, and a Discussion forum where readers can comment on each chapter.

Why you can sign in

Reading is free and needs no account. An account is optional and is only used for the Discussion forum: it gives you a handle that appears beside your comments, so other readers can recognise you across every chapter.

You can create an account with an email address and password, or with Sign in with Google. When you use Google, the site receives only your email address. It never posts anything on your behalf, and it does not access any other Google data.

You can delete your account at any time from Account Settings in the Compendium.

Who runs it

This site is written and run by the author of The Covenant of the Flowers. Questions and requests can be sent through the contact form under Availability on the main site.
