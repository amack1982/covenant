Privacy

The Covenant of the Flowers · updated October 16, 2026

What we collect

Visits. Each visit to the site records an anonymous count: a random ID stored in your browser and the time. No name, email or location.

Reading insights (only if you say yes). How far you read, which chapters, whether sound was on, and an approximate city and country. Nothing that identifies you.

Comments. What you post in Discussion, with your handle (or “Guest”).

Accounts (optional). If you create one: your email address and a handle you choose. Passwords are stored, hashed, by our provider Supabase; we never see them. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address from Google.

How it is used

Only to run the site, show the Discussion, and understand how the work is read. We do not sell, share or advertise with any of it.

Where it is kept

With Supabase, our database provider. Your preferences (music, light or dark, your handle) are also saved in your own browser.

Your choices

You can decline reading insights at any time. Signed-in readers can delete their account from the Account panel on the Compendium contents page: this erases the email, password and handle for good. Past comments remain, shown as “former reader”. Clearing your browser data removes anything stored on your device.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the contact form under Availability on the main site.
