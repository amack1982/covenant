A Note to the Reader
Our Council is not a simple circle! While reading the Covenant of the Flowers, you may ask, 'To whom is the author referring here?' This chart of our real-world social links is thereby provided as your lamp in the storm. There are many other names and appearances in the Covenant of the Flowers. You must discover them yourself.
Additionally, all links are limited to the author's understanding of each relationship at the time of writing.
